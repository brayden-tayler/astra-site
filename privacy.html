Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Astra is a private home assistant used by one household on its own tablet. This policy explains what Astra stores, where, and how to remove it. Astra has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares data.

What Astra stores

Astra's server runs on Fly.io in Sydney, Australia. On that server Astra stores:

  • Household settings the household enters: playback volume, monthly budget, and household members' names, roles and notes.
  • Approximate home location: the tablet shares its location with the server so Astra can show local weather and prepare the morning routine. The tablet rounds the position to about 1 km before it is sent (two decimal places of latitude and longitude), so the server never receives or stores an exact position or street address. Astra also stores the tablet's time zone, when the location was last updated, and the name of the suburb or town that position falls in (shown on the weather card). Astra asks Android for approximate location only, never precise or background location, and the tablet checks when the app opens, when it is used again, and every few hours while it is open.
  • Reminders and routines the household creates.
  • Usage totals for running costs: numbers of tokens, conversations, conversation durations and response times. No conversation content is stored.
  • Paired devices: a name and a one-way hash of each tablet's access key.
  • Google authorisation (only if Google Calendar is connected): the Google refresh token, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is kept separately as a server secret.

The tablet stores its own access key in Android's encrypted secure storage.

What Astra does not store

  • Voice and conversations. During a conversation, audio travels directly from the tablet to OpenAI's Realtime API, and spoken replies come back the same way. Astra does not record or keep audio or transcripts. Speech is transcribed by OpenAI during a conversation; the text is used only in memory and is not stored. OpenAI processes the audio under its privacy policy and API data usage policies.
  • Calendar events. Events stay in Google Calendar. Astra reads them from Google when it needs to show or discuss them, keeps only a temporary in-memory cache while its server is running, and never saves copies to disk.

Google user data

When the household connects a Google account, Astra asks for two permissions:

  • View and edit events on your calendars (calendar.events): to show events and to create, change or delete events when a household member asks.
  • See the list of your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly): to know which calendars exist and which ones can be edited.

Google data is used only to provide these calendar features to the household. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone. When a household member asks Astra about their calendar by voice, the relevant event details are passed to the OpenAI conversation so Astra can answer, and are not stored afterwards.

Astra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • Weather: the approximate (1 km) home coordinates, or those of a city you ask about, are sent to Open-Meteo to get forecasts. City search uses Open-Meteo's GeoNames-based geocoding.
  • Suburb name: when the saved home changes, the server sends the same approximate (1 km) coordinates, and nothing else about you or the household, to Nominatim, the free reverse-geocoding service of OpenStreetMap, to find the suburb or town name. Like any web request, it also reaches the service from the server's network address, which is Fly.io's, not the tablet's. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Hosting: Fly.io hosts Astra's server and its storage, with automatic daily volume snapshots kept for a few days.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Disconnect Google Calendar with Disconnect in Astra's Settings under Google Calendar. Astra asks Google to revoke its access and deletes the stored Google authorisation. You can also remove Astra at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.
  • Household details, reminders and routines can be edited or deleted in Astra, or by asking Astra.
  • Location: turn location off for Astra in Android's settings and the tablet stops sharing it; the last saved approximate location stays on the server until it is replaced or removed by the developer on request.
  • Everything else, including usage totals and paired devices, can be removed by the developer on request. Deleted data also disappears from the automatic snapshots once they expire.
