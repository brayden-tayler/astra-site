Astra Home Assistant

Astra is a private, voice-first home assistant that runs on one household's dedicated Android tablet.

What Astra does

  • Shows the time, date and a simple home dashboard.
  • Holds spoken conversations, started by tapping the on-screen orb.
  • Shows the household's Google Calendar events and can add, change or remove events when asked.
  • Keeps household reminders and daily routines, such as a morning briefing.
  • Gives local weather forecasts.

Who it is for

Astra is a personal project built for a single household. It is not sold, advertised or offered to the public, and it has no user accounts for other people to sign up to.

Google Calendar

If the household connects a Google account, Astra uses Google Calendar only to show that household's calendars and events and to create, update or delete events when a household member asks it to. Calendar events stay in Google Calendar. Read the privacy policy for details, including how to disconnect.
